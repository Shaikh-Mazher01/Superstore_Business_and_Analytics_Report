# Superstore Business & Analytics Report

## Overview
Thirty scenario-based business questions answered against the classic Tableau Superstore dataset, each backed by a chart and a short explanation of why that chart type was the right call for the question.

## Sample of the questions covered
- Which product categories generate the highest total sales, and how is that split distributed?
- How do monthly sales trend across the year, and where are the peaks and dips?
- Can individual customer performance be tracked over time?
- How does sales volume vary by day of week and category?
- How is profit distributed across subcategories within different customer segments?
- What's each region's percentage contribution to overall sales?
- How do profit margins differ across shipping modes and customer segments?
- How long does order processing take by product category?
- How do discounts affect overall profit?
Each answer follows the same format: a direct answer, the chart used to support it, and a short explanation of why that chart type (bar, line, pie, stacked bar, etc.) fit the question better than the alternatives.
