# Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive, enterprise-grade Tableau dashboard designed to solve **30 distinct business scenarios** for global retail operations. This business intelligence asset converts massive historical sales data into an interactive control center, enabling executives to diagnose global supply chain metrics, isolate regional leaks, and analyze customer behaviors.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Core Skills
* **BI Tool:** Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public
* **Data Prep:** Tableau Prep / Excel
* **Core Competencies:** Executive Dashboard Design, KPI Tracking, Geospatial Analysis, Cohort Retention, Data Storytelling

## 🎛️ Dashboard Modules & Features
* **Executive Overview:** High-level KPI cards tracking global sales, total profit, profit margins, and shipping delays.
* **Geospatial Profit Mapping:** Dynamic maps allowing managers to drill down from a global or country level straight into regional ZIP codes to spot negative-margin clusters.
* **Cohort & Scenario Analytics:** Side-by-side comparative views answering 30 strategic business questions, such as identifying low-performing product sub-categories or diagnosing high-return product lines.
